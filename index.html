Before we work together.

What does Uplof do?

Uplof is a founder-led service that connects websites, SEO, Google Business Profile, lead capture, CRM, follow-up, analytics and reviews. The aim is to make the journey from discovery to enquiry easier to manage. You can start with one part or scope a connected setup around your business.

Can I start with just a website or SEO?

Yes. You can start with a website, landing page, SEO or another clearly scoped part of the system. We still look at the surrounding journey so the work has a useful destination. You are not required to purchase every service or replace tools that already suit your business.

What does full SEO management include?

It covers an ongoing plan for search visibility rather than a one-time page check. Your scope defines the audit, keyword and content work, technical changes, on-page improvements, local relevance and reporting. Any off-site work, content volume and implementation responsibilities are agreed explicitly before the engagement starts.

Do I need a new website?

Not necessarily. If the existing site can support the work, we can improve its content, structure and enquiry journey. A rebuild becomes a discussion when the platform or current setup limits what is needed. We review that first rather than assuming every business needs to start again.

Can you connect my existing WhatsApp, forms or CRM?

We review the tools you use and the integrations they support. The goal is a workable capture and follow-up process, not another disconnected login. Provider access, subscription costs, messaging permissions and any technical limitations are confirmed in the scope before an integration is promised or built.

How much does it cost, and is there a long contract?

Cost and commitment depend on the work: a website build, an integration project and ongoing SEO have different needs. Your proposal sets out deliverables, payment terms, third-party costs and any support period. Contract length, cancellation terms and recurring work are agreed before you decide to proceed.

How soon will I see results?

We separate delivery milestones from performance outcomes. A page or enquiry flow can be checked once implemented, while search visibility and customer response need observation over time. Your starting point, market and scope affect the timeline. We agree milestones without promising a fixed date for rankings or leads.

Can you guarantee rankings, enquiries or revenue?

No. We can commit to the agreed work, clear communication and checking the setup we deliver. Search performance, enquiries and sales also depend on demand, competition, your offer and follow-up. We use measurable project goals and report limitations rather than promising a guaranteed commercial result.

Do you also manage paid ads or social media?

Those are not included in the core offer described here. We can plan how traffic from existing campaigns reaches your website and enters the enquiry process. Running paid campaigns or managing social accounts would be a separate discussion, not something automatically bundled into a website, SEO or funnel engagement.

What do you need from me to start?

Your business name, service area, website or Google Business Profile, and a short explanation of the problem are enough for an initial conversation. If we proceed, we will agree the content, access and approvals required. Do not send passwords or private customer records through the enquiry form.
