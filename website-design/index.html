What does a new website cost?

The price depends on the number of pages, content and integrations. Share your brief and we’ll outline a scope and tailored quote. Domain, hosting and any third-party subscriptions are clarified separately so you can see the full cost.

How long will my website take?

We agree a timeline once the scope is clear. Page count, content readiness, integrations and feedback all affect delivery. Your proposal sets out the milestones and what we need from you.

Do I need to write all the content?

No. We can help shape your messaging and write the agreed pages. You provide the facts about your business, service details, and any genuine project photos or customer feedback you have. You review the copy before it goes live.

Can I keep my existing domain?

Yes. We can connect your existing domain to the new website. If you’re replacing a site, we review existing URLs and agree any redirects as part of the migration scope.

Can customers contact me on WhatsApp?

Yes. We can connect your business WhatsApp number, phone and enquiry form. If you need booking software or a CRM, we’ll confirm the integration and any provider costs in your proposal.

Will this guarantee more leads or Google rankings?

No website can guarantee enquiries or rankings. We build the foundations: a clear offer, helpful content, mobile usability and an easier enquiry path. Results also depend on your traffic, market, offer and follow-up.

What happens after launch?

You receive a handover for the agreed setup. If you need regular edits, a CMS or ongoing support, we can include those in the scope. Ownership, account access and recurring costs are agreed before the build.
